   - A floor "server pit" marking: a flat dark-magenta emissive disc or ring (CSGCylinder3D height 0.04 at y 0.02, radius ~7) centred on the spawn so the arena reads as a place.
   - Magenta/red strip lights along the base of `Wall_North` across the arena span and 2-3 omni "modem" lights (no shadows, like `_add_light`) in magenta so the Queen's wings and the player are lit from a different angle than the rest of the mall.

Label3D must face the camera direction (camera looks toward -z and -x from +x+z; a label on the north wall facing +z is readable).
   - 2-4 "server rack" CSG boxes (0.8 x 2.2 x 0.6) with a thin emissive slit strip, placed near the wall so they do not block movement in the fight (keep >= 6 m clear radius around the spawn). Tall boxes must go through `_add_box` so they register as occluders.
2. **Surface variation, cheaply.** Generate ONE shared tile texture at runtime (an `Image` 256x256 with a 2-px lighter grid line every 64 px and faint noise, built once in the builder, wrapped in an `ImageTexture`) and apply it to the floor and mezzanine deck materials with `uv1_triplanar = true` and a `uv1_scale` giving ~2 m tiles; keep albedo contrast subtle (lines no more than ~12 % lighter) so readability of enemies on the floor is unchanged. Walls: a horizontal trim strip (thin emissive dim-cyan CSG box at 2.6 m along `Wall_North`, `Wall_West` and the storefront back walls) and a slightly darker albedo on the upper half is enough; no textures on walls.
3. **Signage elsewhere.** `Label3D` names over the three kiosks (`BEEPER WORLD`, `NEON JULIUS`, `CASSETTE VAULT`) in warm orange/yellow matching their light pools, and `SUBWAY ARCADE` on the SW storefront back wall. Modest size (1-1.5 m text height), unshaded, with outline; they should glow slightly under the existing glow settings, not blow out.
4. **Pillars**: add a base plinth and a top cap ring (CSG, same material) so the colonnade stops looking like extruded cylinders. Mezzanine: one extra `mall_planter.glb` prop via `_add_prop_or_box` on the deck and a `FOOD COURT ->` sign on `Wall_West` above it.
